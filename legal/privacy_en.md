# Privacy Policy and Data Protection
## 1. Audience Measurement (Plausible Analytics)
This site uses the Plausible Analytics audience measurement tool (self-hosted). This tool is configured with strict respect for user privacy:

- It uses no cookies.

- It collects no personal or identifiable data.

- It does not track users across different websites.

- Statistical traffic data is entirely anonymized and aggregated upon collection.

## 2. Local Storage
The site may store temporary or persistent non-personal technical data in your browser's localStorage. This data is used exclusively to ensure the proper technical functioning of the site (for example, saving your game preferences).

## 3. Third-Party Services, CDNs, and External APIs
To ensure proper display and technical operation, the site relies on external resources (such as Content Delivery Networks - CDN, web fonts like Google Fonts, CartoDB base maps, mapping libraries like MapLibre, and APIs such as countries.dev or Wikipedia).
When your browser loads these resources, these third-party services may technically receive basic technical data (such as your IP address) in accordance with their respective privacy policies. No personal data is sold, rented, or transmitted to third parties by the site editor.

## 4. Absence of Personal Data Collection
Since the site does not process user registrations, does not collect email addresses, and does not use advertising trackers, no individual personal data is recorded. Consequently, there are no user profiles to consult, modify, or delete.
